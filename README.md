# Dad Joke Showdown

One dad joke for the make.fun bounty `u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5`.

## The joke

> My son asked why I tell so many dad jokes. I told him it's my puns-ibility as a father.

87 characters. A son asks why his father tells so many dad jokes, and the father answers with the pun itself: it is his "puns-ibility" — his responsibility, as a father, to pun. The joke is the answer, the pun lands cleanly on first read, and it reads naturally aloud across a dinner table.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the exact joke above to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed). It is deterministic: every run produces byte-identical output.
